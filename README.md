## SolCoop

![banner](https://github.com/viviangomesp/SolCoop/blob/main/SolCoop.png)

SolCoop is a Java-based console application designed to encourage collaborative and sustainable use of solar energy. It works as a marketplace for surplus solar energy, connecting those who generate extra energy with those who need it.

The system simulates a cooperative energy network where users can sell, buy, or co-invest in solar energy solutions.

---

#### User Roles
- SunShare: Users who produce solar energy and want to sell their surplus.

- SunSeeker: Users looking to buy surplus energy from others.

- SunParter: Individuals or companies willing to co-own and share costs of solar panel installations.

---

#### Tecnologies Used

- Language: Java

- Interface: Command-line (console)

- Programming Paradigm: Object-Oriented Programming

- Data Persistence: Simulated with internal structures (no database)
